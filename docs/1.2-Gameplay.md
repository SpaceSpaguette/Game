# Gameplay


    Our game has a 3 stage system.
## Stage 0
    This is the stage the game starts in. This serves as a sort of a tutorial. Not complex by any means. Introduces the player to basic mechanics and attempts to teach them about what is availible
    
    Initial stage is peaceful and guided. Player needs to navigate an NPC through the facility. His mission is to lock doors. open them. close them. Or do a scan of a room

## Stage 1 - Early Game
    This stage begins after the tutorial is complete. In timeline its placed about a week after the tutorial. Game begins with alerts and errors in containment. Player has to navigate open and close doors to navigate scientists into saferoms/shelters. Some have a higher priority in which case player has to decide if he helps them escape or not. AKA Director

    Stage two involves anomalies and scans. Player needs to vigilant otherwise they loose important assets. If scientists die in certain ways 008 or 049. The player should minimize the looses of scientists. He can also get security guards through the game and navigate them. They are early game NPC which can neutralize some anomalies.

    This stage is very very focused on minimizing losses to prevent late game drag.

## Stage 2 - Mid Game
    This stage begins once mtf arrives to the facility. Player is then instructed to help them stabilize the facility. Player can call in a task force with custom loadout and a special task forces as well.

    The enemies of the player are anomalies, Class D and Chaos Insurgency. This is also where the player's saving of personal comes in handy. The more personale that was saved helps the player both unclock new abilities or augment current mtf squads. This comes in pretty handy.
